      A clear route to the keys

      From your first message to your front door.

      01
      MessageDay 1 MatchDay 2–3 ReserveDay 4 LandArrival day
      1. 01

        Message

        Tell us your university, your start date, your monthly budget and whether you need a desk. Two minutes on WhatsApp, in your own language.

      2. 02

        Match

        We shortlist by walking time to your faculty gate — not by district name — and send video walkthroughs so you can judge them from home.

      3. 03

        Reserve

        We reconfirm the room is still available, explain the deposit and the lease in plain English, and hold it against your arrival date.

      4. 04

        Land

        Someone meets you with the keys. If the room is not what the video showed, you do not take it and you do not lose the deposit.
