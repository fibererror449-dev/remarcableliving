# Boutique real-estate homepage section research

Research date: 28 August 2026

## What the strongest reference sites repeatedly do

- The Modern House treats property discovery as editorial curation and supports it with a substantial journal, while its FAQ makes the buying process and service boundaries easy to inspect.
- Fantastic Frank leads with a curated global collection, then mixes timely picks, editor selections, journal stories, and a direct contact invitation.
- Knight Frank combines property search with area guides and research so a visitor can understand a place and market before contacting an adviser.
- Christie's International Real Estate combines destination and lifestyle discovery with market perspectives, editorial home tours, and a clear advisory path.

The transferable principle is not their visual styling. It is the information sequence:

1. Establish a point of view.
2. Let visitors discover a manageable collection.
3. Help them choose a place, not only a unit.
4. Explain what happens after they make contact.
5. Offer useful editorial context and one clear personal next step.

## Sections implemented for REMARCABLE LIVING

### Explore by neighbourhood

Four area cards act as real filters for the current residence collection. Counts come from the loaded listing data, not marketing copy. The descriptions tell visitors what to compare without asserting unverified lifestyle, distance, or market claims.

### From brief to the front door

The service journey makes the existing promise concrete: Brief, Compare, Confirm, View. It explicitly reconfirms availability and asking rent before a visit and does not promise access, negotiation outcomes, legal work, or inventory that the source data cannot prove.

## Deliberately not added

- Testimonials without attributable customer approval
- Awards, press logos, transaction totals, years of experience, or market-share claims without evidence
- Invented neighbourhood superlatives or precise commute claims
- An off-market inventory promise
- Generic luxury icon rows that add decoration but no decision support
